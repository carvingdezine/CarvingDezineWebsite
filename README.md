## What the studio does

Five disciplines, one standard.

- **UX & UI design:** human-centred interfaces, from first wireframe to final handoff. Product design, design systems, prototyping.
- **Brand identity:** logos, type, colour and guidelines that say who you are at a glance.
- **Presentation design:** pitch decks where every slide earns its place. Keynote and PowerPoint.
- **Print & marketing:** brochures, catalogues and annual reports that people keep.
- **Interactive web:** experimental interfaces where UX, motion and front-end come together. WebGL, motion, front-end.

### Free design health check

Before designing anything, we find out what is not working. It is a plain-English look at your website and brand: what is working, what to improve and what to do next. Every check gets a personal reply within one business day. A sample report is in [`samples/`](samples/ux-audit-sample.html).

### Brand Centre

A brand guide you can actually use: your guidelines, logo files and ready-to-use templates in one toolkit made for your company. Options and details are on the [Brand Centre page](https://carvingdezine.com/brand-centre).

### Selected clients

Mobcast, ICICI Bank, UFO Movies and LifeLine Hospital, among others. The full portfolio is at [carvingdezine.com/work](https://carvingdezine.com/work).

## About this website

The site is hand-built rather than made from a template. It is designed to feel like the work it showcases: editorial layout, careful type, and motion used with purpose.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Home: services, free health check, selected work, about, FAQ and contact |
| `work.html` | The full portfolio, filterable by category and client |
| `project.html` | Individual project case studies |
| `brand-centre.html` | The Brand Centre offering |
| `client.html` | A private page where clients open their own files with a personal link and PIN |
| `samples/` | A sample design health-check report |
| `assets/` | Shared styles and scripts |

### Built with

- Plain **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**, with no front-end framework
- **GSAP** for animation
- **Cloudflare Pages** for hosting, with Cloudflare Functions for the portfolio and client portal
- **PostgreSQL (Neon)** for portfolio content

### Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser to see the static pages. The portfolio and client portal need the hosted backend, so those parts will not load from a plain local file.
