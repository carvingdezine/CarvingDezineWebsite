Questions

Good to know before we start.

What is a Brand Centre, and how is it different from a brand guideline PDF?

A Brand Centre is your brand guidelines and a ready set of on-brand tools in one toolkit, made for your company. Instead of a PDF that people read once, your team gets templates for posts, cards, brochures and decks that already follow the rules.

Do I need to sign in or install anything?

No. There are no logins and no platform to sign in to. Your Brand Centre opens in any modern browser.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo files (SVG is best), any brand notes, and your colours and fonts if you have them. If you don't, the Essential and Complete options build them for you.

What does each option include, and what does it cost?

Starter (₹49,000 in India, $1,200 overseas): you already have a logo and brand, and I set up your Brand Centre with guidelines and 10 templates. Essential (₹99,000, $2,400): everything in Starter, plus a brand system built for you and 15 templates. Complete (₹1,79,000, $4,200): everything in Essential, plus your logo and brand designed from scratch, a print kit, motion and video exports and 25 templates.

Can my team edit the templates?

Yes. Your team changes the words and images in a template and exports the result, while the logo, colours and type stay on brand.

What logo files will I get?

Your logo as SVG, PNG, JPEG and WebP for screens, favicons and app icons, CMYK EPS and PDF for printers, one-colour versions, and a PDF logo guide, organised in folders. Print files are made from a clean SVG of your logo.

What happens after launch, and who owns the Brand Centre?

It's made for your company and it's yours to keep, so it keeps working whether or not you pay for updates. Updates and fixes after launch are ₹15,000 a year ($300 overseas), or you can buy small update packs when you need them.

How long does it take?

You get a clear timeline before we start, based on the option you choose and how much you already have in place.
