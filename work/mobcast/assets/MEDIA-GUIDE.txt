-------------------------------------------------
1. RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERYTHING
-------------------------------------------------
PHOTOS
- Format: JPG (sRGB). Use PNG only for logos or screenshots with sharp text.
- Size: export at 2x the size shown on screen so it stays sharp on phones and laptops. The pixel sizes in the tables below already include this.
- File size: stay under the "max" in the tables. Compress with squoosh.app or tinypng.com (quality 75 to 82 is enough). A page that loads fast ranks and converts better.
- Orientation: follow the ratio in the table. If a photo is a different ratio the page crops it from the centre, so keep faces and the subject near the middle.
- Never upload photos with: visible customer names, ID cards, account numbers, phone screens with real data, competitor logos, or watermarks (Shutterstock, Getty, etc.).
- People: use real Mobcast people and customers wherever possible. Every person who is clearly visible needs to have agreed (a short photo consent message is enough). For stock photos, check the licence allows commercial website use without attribution.

VIDEOS
- Format: MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio. This plays on every browser and phone.
- Keep it short and light. Aim for the "max" sizes below. Use HandBrake (free): preset "Fast 1080p30", RF 24 to 26, tick "Web optimized".
- No autoplay with sound. Videos with sound start only when the visitor presses play.
- Always add a poster image (a still frame) so the page looks right before the video loads.
- Record in landscape unless the table says portrait. Steady shots, good light, clean audio (a phone mic held close, or a clip-on mic).

AUDIO
- MP3, 128 kbps, mono, about 10 seconds, under 200 KB per file.

STOCK SEARCH TIPS
- Good free libraries: Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay (all allow commercial use, check each image). Paid: Adobe Stock, Envato.
- Search in English and add "India" or "Indian" plus the setting. Add "candid" or "natural light" to avoid stiff posed shots.
- Prefer warm daylight, real offices and branches, mobile phones in hand. Avoid: generic handshake shots, people pointing at glowing holograms, clip-art style illustrations.
- The site palette is warm orange and ink black. Pictures with warm light or neutral backgrounds sit best. Avoid strongly blue or green tinted photos.
