FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this a replacement for our existing LMS?

It can be. Many teams use Frontline LXP as the frontline learning layer on top of, or instead of, an existing LMS. We will map it to your setup in the demo.

Can we bring our existing content (SCORM, xAPI)?

Yes. SCORM and xAPI are supported, so your existing content moves in as it is.

Will it work for employees without a desk?

That is what it is built for. It is a mobile-first app designed for the branch, store, plant and field, with short lessons that fit between customers and shifts.

Which languages are supported?

Content and interface are available in each employee's own language. Tell us the languages you need and we will confirm them in your demo.

Can the app carry our brand and sit in our app-store account?

Yes. Starter carries your logo and two brand colours, Professional adds a co-branded app, and Enterprise is fully white-labelled on your own store accounts.

How does it connect to our HRMS and SSO?

SSO (SAML 2.0 / OIDC), Active Directory / LDAP, REST APIs and webhooks are available on Professional and Enterprise, with HRMS and ERP integration handled in the Connect step of the rollout.

Where is our data hosted and how is it secured?

Data is hosted in India by default, with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Read more on our Security page.

How fast can we go live?

As little as 3 working days on Starter, 7 on Professional and 15 on Enterprise.
