START HERE - Carving Dezine
============================

Keep this file on your Desktop. Do NOT put it inside the website folder.

There are only 5 jobs. Do them in this order. After each job, check it
worked before moving on. You do not need to understand anything else.

Two words you will see:
  ADMIN_KEY    = your password. You invent it. You type it to sign in.
  DATABASE_URL = the address of your database. You copy it once.


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 1 - Check two settings in Cloudflare            (about 5 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com and log in.
2. Click "Workers & Pages".
3. Click your website.
4. Click "Settings".
5. Click "Variables and Secrets" (it may say "Environment variables").
6. Look for two names in the list: ADMIN_KEY and DATABASE_URL.

What you find, and what to do:

  Both are there:
    Your database is already set up. Skip JOB 2.
    The old admin page was public, so make a NEW password now:
    click "Edit" next to ADMIN_KEY, delete the old value, type a new
    long password (20 or more letters and numbers, no spaces), click Save.
    WRITE THIS PASSWORD DOWN somewhere safe. A password manager is best.

  ADMIN_KEY is missing:
    Click "Add". Name: ADMIN_KEY. Value: a long password (20 or more
    letters and numbers, no spaces). Choose "Secret" or "Encrypt" if it
    asks. Click Save. WRITE THE PASSWORD DOWN.

  DATABASE_URL is missing:
    You need to create the database. Go to JOB 2.

Check: you can see both names in the list. (You cannot see the values.
That is normal.)


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 2 - Create the database      ONLY if DATABASE_URL was missing
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Go to neon.tech and sign up (it is free).
2. Click "Create project". Name it carvingdezine. Pick the region
   closest to India.
3. On the project page click "Connect". Copy the long address that
   starts with postgresql://
4. Go back to Cloudflare (JOB 1, steps 1 to 5). Click "Add".
   Name: DATABASE_URL. Value: paste the address. Click Save.
5. Back in Neon click "SQL Editor".
6. Open these 3 files with Notepad, one at a time, in this order.
   For each one: select all (Ctrl+A), copy (Ctrl+C), click inside
   the Neon SQL Editor, paste (Ctrl+V), click "Run".
     a) D:\Github\CarvingDezineWebsite\schema.sql
     b) D:\Github\CarvingDezineWebsite\001_client_portal.sql
     c) D:\Github\CarvingDezineWebsite\sql\002_portal_hardening.sql

Check: each file says it ran successfully, with no red error.

(If your portfolio projects were lost, they come back later in the
workspace: Portfolio backup, then Restore, using your projects.json.)


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 3 - Put the new files on your website          (about 10 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open site-update.zip. Select everything inside and copy it.
2. Open the folder D:\Github\CarvingDezineWebsite
3. Paste. When Windows asks, click "Replace the files in the
   destination". The folders "assets" and "functions" will merge
   with your existing ones. That is correct.
4. Open GitHub Desktop. You will see a list of changed files.
5. In the small box at the bottom left type: workspace update
6. Click "Commit to main".
7. Click "Push origin" at the top.
8. Wait 2 minutes. In Cloudflare, click Workers & Pages, your website,
   then "Deployments". The newest one should say "Success".

Check: the top deployment says Success.


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 4 - Sign in and test                           (about 5 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. In your browser go to: carvingdezine.com/admin
2. You should see a black page that says "Private area".
3. Type your ADMIN_KEY password and click "Sign in".
4. Your workspace opens. Click "Manage projects" on the left. Your
   portfolio projects should be listed.

Now the safety test (this proves strangers cannot get in):
5. Open a private window (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome). Do NOT sign in there.
6. Go to carvingdezine.com/api/session
   It must show:  {"signedIn":false}
   If you see "Not found" instead, the new files did not publish: do
   JOB 3 again.
7. Go to carvingdezine.com/admin.html
   It must show the sign-in page. It must NOT show your workspace.
8. Go to carvingdezine.com/schema.sql
   It must say "Not found".

You stay signed in only until you close your browser. If you tick
"Keep me signed in", it is 7 days. The workspace also signs you out
after 60 minutes of doing nothing. Use "Sign out" when you finish.

If any of this is different, take a screenshot and send it to me.
Say which JOB and which step you were on.

Wrong password? It is exactly the ADMIN_KEY value in Cloudflare.
Lost it? Do JOB 1 again with a new password, wait 2 minutes, try again.


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 5 - Load your earnings                          (about 3 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. In the workspace click "Earnings" on the left.
2. Click "Import Projects.xlsx" and choose your file.
3. A check screen appears. Look at it, then click "Save to database".

Important: keep Projects.xlsx OUT of the GitHub folder. It contains
client emails.


----------------------------------------------------------------
JOB 6 - Make your GitHub folder private           (about 3 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

Your GitHub folder (repository) is public. Your earnings and client
details are NOT in it, they live in your database. But the workspace
code is, and it is better kept private.

1. Go to github.com and open your carvingdezine repository.
2. Click "Settings" (top row of the repository).
3. Scroll to the very bottom, to "Danger Zone".
4. Click "Change repository visibility", then "Make private".
   Follow the prompts and type the repository name to confirm.
5. Wait 2 minutes. In Cloudflare, Workers & Pages, your website,
   Deployments: make a small change and push, and check it still
   publishes. If it does not, in Cloudflare open Settings, Builds,
   and reconnect the GitHub account when it asks.

Also look inside the folder called "website" in your project. If it holds
old copies of your pages, tell me what is in it before you publish again.
Do not delete it yet.

Also never put these in the folder: Projects.xlsx, passwords, the
DATABASE_URL address, invoices, or client files.
