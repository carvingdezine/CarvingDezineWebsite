Questions

Good to know before we start.

Working together

What do you need from me to get started?

A brief explaining what you need, who it's for, your timeline and any references you like. The more context you give me, the better the outcome. Everything is remote, through email, calls and shared tools like Figma, Notion and Google Drive, and some of my best work has been for early-stage startups and growing businesses.

How long does a project take, and how many revisions are included?

A presentation deck takes 3 to 5 days, a brand identity 2 to 4 weeks and a full UX project 4 to 8 weeks. You get a clear timeline before we start. Every project includes 2 to 3 structured revision rounds, and I prefer clear, consolidated feedback over scattered small changes.

What do I receive, and who owns it?

All production-ready formats for your project, such as AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, PNG, JPG, PPTX and Figma files. You own everything, with no locked files and no ongoing dependencies.

Project or monthly: which should I choose?

Choose a project if you need one thing done properly, with a fixed scope and price. Choose monthly if you expect a steady stream of requests. It's one flat rate, and you can pause or cancel anytime.

How does payment work?

I invoice directly: Wise for international clients and UPI for India. No card details and no third-party checkout.

Free health check and audits

What do I get from the free design health check?

A short written note from me, within one business day, on your website and brand: what's working, what to improve and what to do first. It's free and there's no obligation to hire me afterwards. It's a first look from the outside, so it covers the big things, not every screen.

What's the difference between the free check and a paid audit?

The free check is a quick, plain-English first look. A paid audit goes through your product in depth, screen by screen, with every finding tied to a priority and a realistic first phase you can hand to a designer or developer. You can read a sample audit, and ask me for a quote when you're ready.

Brand Centre

What is a Brand Centre, and how is it different from a brand guideline PDF?

A Brand Centre is your brand guidelines and a ready set of on-brand tools in one toolkit, made for your company. Instead of a PDF that people read once, your team gets templates for posts, cards, brochures and decks that already follow the rules. There are no logins and no platform to sign in to.

What does each Brand Centre option include, and what does it cost?

Starter (₹49,000 in India, $1,200 overseas): you already have a logo and brand, and I set up your Brand Centre with guidelines and 10 templates. Essential (₹99,000, $2,400): everything in Starter, plus a brand system built for you and 15 templates. Complete (₹1,79,000, $4,200): everything in Essential, plus your logo and brand designed from scratch, a print kit, motion and video exports and 25 templates. Prices are set separately for India and overseas, not converted.

What happens after launch, and who owns the Brand Centre?

It's made for your company and it's yours to keep, so it keeps working whether or not you pay for updates. Updates and fixes after launch are ₹15,000 a year ($300 overseas), or you can buy small update packs when you need them.
